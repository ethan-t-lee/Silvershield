Safety Reminders

Use these quick reminders while you practice.

  • Never share one-time codes, passwords, or PINs over the phone.
  • Hang up and call the organization back using an official number you found yourself.
  • Urgency and pressure are common warning signs in scam calls.
  • If a caller claims to be family, verify with another trusted person first.
